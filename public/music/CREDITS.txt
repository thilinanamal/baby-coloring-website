Music — public-domain nursery-rhyme melodies, rendered in-house.

Twinkle Twinkle, Old MacDonald, London Bridge, Mary Had a Little Lamb,
Row Row Row Your Boat, and The Wheels on the Bus are traditional
public-domain melodies. The audio here was generated from those melodies
with fluidsynth (Music Box instrument) — no third-party recording is
used, so no attribution is required.

To regenerate or add songs: edit SONGS in scripts/render-music.mjs, then
  SF2=/path/to/gm.sf2 node scripts/render-music.mjs
  node scripts/build-music.mjs
